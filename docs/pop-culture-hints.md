# Pop-culture hint coverage

Reviewed on 9 October 2026. The current answer pool contains 129 animals, including
73 members of Dinosauria (birds included). The database has 25 pop-culture clues
across 22 dinosaur profiles. The other 51 eligible dinosaurs remain without a
pop-culture clue; the 56 non-dinosaur answers were outside this research pass.
This is a selected set of useful references, not an exhaustive appearance catalogue.
Lack of a clue is never evidence that an animal has not appeared in a work.

The complete per-animal status is generated in `animal-clue-coverage.json`.
The actual wording, category, screen-work title, scope, topic and source URLs live
in `public/data/animal-clues.json`. Existing scientific clues remain unchanged.

## The Land Before Time TV series

Four additional character clues were checked against the TV series, rather than
assuming that a film character also appeared on television:

- **Triceratops (439):** Cera.
- **Stegosaurus (359):** Spike.
- **Tyrannosaurus (156):** Chomper.
- **Oviraptor (265):** Ruby, described as feathered in the TV review.

[Amblin's TV page](https://amblin.com/tv/land-before-time/) confirms all four
characters in the series. Its [franchise character guide](https://amblin.com/movie/the-land-before-time/)
identifies Cera, Spike and Chomper's genera. The [Common Sense Media TV review](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/tv-reviews/the-land-before-time)
corroborates those identifications and identifies Ruby as an Oviraptor.
Each clue carries its own source links in the database.

Ducky and Petrie are deferred: the reviewed pages use inconsistent or imprecise
animal names. Littlefoot is also deferred pending resolution of the Apatosaurus /
Brontosaurus naming across franchise material, since both are separate game answers.
No uncertain identification is used to eliminate another candidate.

## Editorial boundaries and remaining coverage

- Do not transfer a portrayal between related genera, even when the screen animal's
  anatomy resembles another genus. Generic labels such as “raptor” and “longneck”
  are insufficient by themselves.
- The initial pass favours distinct characters and scenes. Mere presence in a large
  movie/game roster is not a useful clue. For example, the Frontier announcement
  confirms an Ouranosaurus variant but does not supply a distinctive scene for it;
  no clue was added from that fact alone.
- None of the new clues has automatic candidate exclusions. Unlisted screen
  appearances and unsourced comparisons remain unknown.
- A clue can be in the database yet unavailable after the player's guesses/name
  clues leave only one candidate. The existing usefulness filter still applies.
- These references favour English-language film/TV and the Jurassic franchise.
  Other languages, games, books and less familiar dinosaurs need a separate research
  pass. Player familiarity and clue difficulty still need gameplay feedback.

## Selection and saves

Both categories share the Factual Hint button and the shared first-free allowance.
The small Scientific/Pop culture label appears in the journal and result sources:
green for Scientific and dark ochre for Pop culture, with the wording retained so
colour is not the only distinction.
Seeded clue priorities replace the previous fixed ordering; category has no sorting
weight. Used topics and unhelpful facts remain filtered out. Daily starts from the
same order for the same date and answer; Endless saves a new random seed per round.
Reloads preserve the seed, purchased clues, original prices and free allowance.

These releases only add clues, so version `2026-10-09.4` explicitly keeps versions
`2026-10-09.2` and `2026-10-09.3` compatible. Future edits to existing facts must re-evaluate that declaration rather
than silently changing what a player already bought.
